{# The speed bar, on every page that speaks (lessons.html, phonetics.html), and driven by speech-speed.js. Include it only with voice on: both knobs act on sounds /api/tts makes, and it answers 503 without a voice. Two knobs, because they are two different things. "Voice speed" is Kokoro's own speaking rate, sent with each /api/tts request: the voice really says the words slower, so it sounds natural even slow, but only sentences synthesised after the change hear it. "Playback" is the browser's playbackRate on the finished sound: it takes effect at once, on what is already playing too, at the cost of a stretched sound at the slow end. They multiply. The word highlighting follows the audio's own clock, so it stays in step under both. #}
