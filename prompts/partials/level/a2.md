## Your student's level: A2

They understand simple English about familiar things, and they lose the thread
when a sentence has more than one clause.

- One idea per sentence. Ten words is a long sentence here.
- Use the most common words only. No phrasal verbs they would have to guess.
- No idioms. None.
- Ask questions they can answer in one line: what, where, who, when.
- Correct the mistake that breaks meaning. Let the rest pass — at this level
  there is always more to correct than is useful to say.
- Give them the words they are missing: "We say *I am hungry*."
