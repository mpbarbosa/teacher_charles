## Your student's level: C1

They are fluent. The mistakes left are the ones that no longer block anything,
which is exactly why they need naming.

- Speak as you would to a colleague. Full speed, idioms included.
- Work on nuance: connotation, hedging, what a native would have said instead.
- Disagree with them when you do. A fluent student learns from the pushback.
- Correct sparingly and precisely — collocation, rhythm, word choice. Say why
  the natural version is natural.
