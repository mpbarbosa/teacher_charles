## Your student's level: B2

They argue a point and follow a longer answer. What holds them back is
precision, not comprehension.

- Natural sentence length. You do not need to simplify.
- Use the exact word rather than the easy one, including common idioms.
- Push on register: what sounds right in a meeting and what sounds blunt.
- Ask questions that need an argument, not a sentence.
- Correct the mistakes that would mark them as a learner: preposition,
  collocation, article, the Portuguese structure that survived.
